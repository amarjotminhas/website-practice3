About

The carpenter behind the company

Who you're hiring, and how Everwood Construction got here.

Dan Kowalski, owner

Dan Kowalski grew up on job sites. His father framed houses across the Willamette Valley for thirty years, and Dan spent his summers hauling lumber and learning why a wall goes up the way it does. He started Everwood Construction in 2004 with one truck and a reputation for showing up when he said he would.

More than 480 projects later, that hasn't changed. Dan still walks every estimate himself, writes the scope he's willing to stand behind, and runs a small crew that does the work in-house rather than handing it to whoever is cheapest that month. When something goes wrong behind a wall — and on older Portland homes, something usually does — you hear about it from him, in plain language, with a written number before any extra work starts.

The company is licensed as Oregon CCB #198442, bonded, and insured, and every job carries a 5-year workmanship warranty. That warranty isn't marketing; it's Dan agreeing to come back and fix what his crew built if it fails.

There's no showroom and no sales team here. You get the person whose name is on the license, from the first estimate to the final walkthrough.

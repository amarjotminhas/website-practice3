Our work

Six recent British Columbia projects

Each one lists the real budget range, the schedule, and the specific challenge we solved. Specifics are the point.

Kitchen remodel

Laurelhurst

$52–68k·7 weeks

The 1912 house had no way to open the kitchen to the dining room without a post in the middle of the walkway. We set a flush LVL beam up inside the ceiling so the span carried clean, then re-ran the gas line for the new range wall.

Bathroom renovation

Irvington

$28–36k·5 weeks

Once we pulled the old tile we found rot in the subfloor and a corroded cast-iron waste line. We stopped, took photos, and walked the owners through a fixed add of $2,400 before touching anything else.

Room addition

Beaumont

$110–140k·16 weeks

A 320 sq ft primary suite over a new slab. The tricky part was tying the new roofline into the existing gable so it read as original, and stepping the footing for a lot that dropped 3 feet front to back.

Full-home renovation

Sellwood

$210–260k·22 weeks

A full gut of a 1920s bungalow. We pulled all the knob-and-tube wiring, re-leveled a floor that had dropped 1.5 inches over the years, and kept the original fir trim by numbering and storing every piece before demo.

Deck & covered structure

Mt. Tabor

$24–32k·4 weeks

A covered cedar deck on a lot that sloped hard toward the house. We poured six deep footings below frost line, sloped the framing a quarter inch to shed water, and ran a French drain so the grade behind it stays dry.

Structural repair

St. Johns

$34–48k·6 weeks

The back corner of the house was sinking. We shored the floor, replaced two failing support posts and a rotted girder, sistered eleven joists, and set new concrete piers to bring the floor back within a quarter inch of level.

FAQ

Answers before you ask

Do I need permits, and who pulls them?

Most structural, electrical, and plumbing work in Portland needs a permit, and we pull them under our license (Oregon CCB #198442). A typical residential building permit runs $300 to $1,200 depending on scope and valuation. We don't start permitted work without the approved permit in hand.

How does payment work?

We bill in stages tied to progress, not calendar dates. A typical schedule is a deposit to reserve your slot, then draws at framing, rough-in, and substantial completion, with the final payment after your walkthrough. We never ask for the full amount up front.

How accurate are your timelines?

The written timeline is our honest estimate, and we hit it most of the time. Delays usually come from two places: permit review and inspection scheduling, and special-order materials like custom cabinets, which can run 6 to 10 weeks. We flag both early rather than at the end.

What if I want to change something mid-project?

Changes happen. Any change to the scope gets its own written change order with the added cost and time spelled out before we do the work. Nothing lands on your bill that you didn't sign off on first.

What does the warranty cover?

Our 5-year workmanship warranty covers the labor and installation we performed — if something we built fails, we come back and fix it. Manufacturer warranties on products like appliances and windows are separate, and we register those in your name.

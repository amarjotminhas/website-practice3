Services

What we build and repair

Six kinds of residential work, handled in-house from permit to punch list.

Kitchen remodels

Layout changes, new cabinets and counters, moving plumbing and gas, and opening walls where the framing allows. We pull the permits and schedule the inspections so you don't have to.

Bathroom renovations

Full tear-outs down to the studs, waterproofing done right, and new plumbing and tile. We check for hidden rot and old waste lines before we quote the finish work.

Room additions

Bump-outs, primary suites, and second stories. New foundation, framing that ties into your existing roofline, and finishes chosen to match the original house.

Full-home renovations

Whole-house updates: wiring, plumbing, insulation, floors, and finishes. We sequence the work and send a short update every week so you know what's happening.

Decks & outdoor structures

Cedar and composite decks, covered patios, and pergolas. Footings poured below frost line and flashing that keeps water off the house, not just a pretty surface.

Structural repair

Foundation fixes, failing beams and posts, sagging floors, and dry rot. We shore the structure first, then repair it, and we show you exactly what we found.

Not sure which bucket your project fits? Describe it and we'll tell you straight. Ask about your project.
